Executive Summary

Transfer pricing in Israel concerns how international transactions between parties with special relationships are priced and evidenced for Israeli income tax purposes under market terms. Section 85A of the Income Tax Ordinance [New Version] 5721-1961 requires an international transaction between related parties to be reported and taxed according to market terms where the special relationship results in a lower profit than would have been realised between independent parties.

In operational terms, Israel applies a detailed taxpayer-level documentation obligation. Every international related-party transaction must be supported by a contemporaneous market conditions study, commonly functioning as the Israeli local transfer pricing file. The study must follow the 2006 Income Tax Regulations (Determination of Market Conditions), covering transaction description, parties, contractual terms, functional analysis, method selection, comparable search, arm's length calculation and supporting financial information.

Israel is distinguished by annual Form 1385 reporting. The taxpayer attaches Form 1385 to the corporate income tax return to disclose international related-party transactions and sign a declaration that the transactions are conducted on market terms. Form 1485 may also apply in certain cases. The Form 1385 declaration should be supported by an economically valid, contemporaneous transfer pricing analysis. The Israel Tax Authority may request the local study and documentation, which must be delivered within 30 days after an authority request under the post-2022 documentation framework.

Israel also applies OECD BEPS Action 13 master file and CbCR rules. A master file is required where the Israeli taxpayer belongs to a multinational group with preceding-year consolidated revenue above ILS 150 million, while Israeli ultimate parents with consolidated group revenue above ILS 3.4 billion must file a country-by-country report. Israel offers unilateral, bilateral and multilateral APA routes, rulings and MAP. This makes Israeli transfer pricing particularly important for technology, software, cybersecurity, life sciences, R&D, semiconductor, IP, venture-backed and multinational group structures.

Purpose

The purpose of the transfer pricing function is to ensure that international related-party transactions connected to Israel are conducted on market terms and can be explained through credible facts, a contemporaneous market conditions study, Form 1385 disclosure and supporting documentation.

It exists to reduce income tax adjustment risk, support the taxpayer's burden of proof, ensure Form 1385 is supported by valid economic analysis, improve Israel Tax Authority audit readiness and provide a coherent basis for APA, MAP and cross-border profit allocation management.

Primary Outcome

A defensible Israeli transfer pricing position in which the relevant international related-party transactions, method, comparable support, market conditions study, Form 1385, master file, local file, CbCR position and cross-border coordination are aligned with market terms and the actual conduct of the parties.

Process Flow

The process flow explains how Israeli transfer pricing work usually progresses from international transaction mapping to market conditions study, annual Form 1385 reporting and potential Tax Authority engagement. It matters because Israel requires transaction-level market terms support for every international related-party transaction, with an Action 13 overlay for qualifying groups.

1. International Transaction MappingIdentify international transactions between parties with special relationships connected to Israel, including R&D, services, goods, financing, licensing, IP, distribution and permanent establishment dealings.
2. Functional AnalysisAnalyse actual functions, assets, risks, contractual terms, value creation, R&D contribution and intangible development of each related party.
3. Method Selection and Comparable ReviewChoose the most appropriate method under the Israeli method hierarchy, conduct comparable search and prepare market terms calculations.
4. Market Conditions Study BuildPrepare the contemporaneous local transfer pricing study required for each international related-party transaction, including the prescribed analysis and supporting financial records.
5. Master File and CbCR AssessmentDetermine whether the ILS 150 million master-file threshold or ILS 3.4 billion CbCR threshold applies to the multinational group.
6. Form 1385 and Tax Return FilingComplete Form 1385, and Form 1485 where relevant, with the corporate income tax return and ensure the declaration of market terms matches the study.
7. ITA Audit, APA or MAP RouteIf uncertainty or controversy arises, provide the requested study and documents within 30 days or pursue unilateral, bilateral or multilateral APA, ruling or treaty MAP as relevant.
Typical OutputsForm 1385, Form 1485 where applicable, market conditions study, local file, master file, CbCR support, functional analysis, benchmarking, intercompany agreements, audit response papers and APA documentation.
Decision Tree

The decision tree simplifies threshold questions that commonly determine the right Israeli transfer pricing approach.

  1. Identify whether the arrangement is an international transaction between parties with special relationships and has Israeli income tax relevance.
  2. Confirm the actual functions, assets, risks, R&D contribution, intangible ownership and contractual terms of each party.
  3. Recognise that every international related-party transaction requires a contemporaneous market conditions study, regardless of master-file or CbCR revenue thresholds.
  4. Assess whether preceding-year group consolidated revenue exceeds ILS 150 million, requiring master-file documentation, or ILS 3.4 billion, requiring CbCR.
  5. Choose the most appropriate method, complete the market conditions study and reconcile the result with Form 1385 and the corporate income tax return.
  6. Decide whether ordinary documentation is sufficient or whether unilateral, bilateral or multilateral APA, ruling or MAP planning is appropriate for material recurring arrangements.

FAQ

The FAQ section collects recurring threshold questions in a concise handbook format.

Does Israel Apply the Arm's Length Principle to International Related-Party Transactions?Yes. Israel applies the arm's length principle through Section 85A of the Income Tax Ordinance and the Income Tax Regulations (Determination of Market Conditions), 2006.
What Is Form 1385?Form 1385 is the annual transfer pricing declaration attached to the Israeli corporate income tax return. It reports international related-party transactions and includes a declaration that transactions are conducted at market terms.
When Is an Israeli Local File Required?A contemporaneous local transfer pricing study is required for every international transaction between parties with special relationships. It must support the market-terms position reported in Form 1385.
When Is an Israeli Master File Required?A master file is required where the Israeli taxpayer belongs to an MNE group with consolidated revenue above ILS 150 million in the preceding year.
When Must Documentation Be Submitted to the Israel Tax Authority?Required local and master file documentation must generally be delivered within 30 days after an authority request under the post-2022 documentation framework.
Does Israel Offer APAs?Yes. Section 85A allows advance certification of market terms, and Israel offers unilateral, bilateral and multilateral APA routes as well as rulings and MAP procedures.
